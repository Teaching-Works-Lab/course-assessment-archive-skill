## Grade Management Requirements

- Total course grades may be recorded by percentage, five-level, or pass/fail systems.
- Exam-course grades generally use percentage scores; assessment/check courses may use percentage, five-level, or pass/fail grades.
- Total grades must be calculated according to the syllabus-required assessment method and component proportions.
- Student grade sheets include:
  - `历年学习成绩表`, recording the highest assessment result for retaken courses when applicable;
  - `课程考核过程汇总表`, truthfully recording assessment process and grades.
- If a student is disqualified from an exam, absent without procedure, or disciplined for misconduct, the course should be marked according to the regulation, while normal process grades should still be entered truthfully where required.

## Process Assessment Archive Principle

Chapter 9 states that course assessment materials are divided into:

- process assessment materials;
- final/terminal assessment materials.

All must be archived according to regulation.

Key principle:

`考核必有依据，评分必有记录`

All process assessment links included in the final course grade must retain verifiable original materials or records as support. Archive materials should truthfully reflect the original teaching and student assessment process. Fabrication in any form is prohibited.

## Process Assessment Common Materials

For all non-thesis courses, process assessment materials should include common materials:

1. Teaching syllabus.
   - The syllabus should clearly state process assessment requirements in the relevant assessment section.
2. Grade determination record sheet.
   - It should clearly record each student's process assessment grades and total grade original scoring record.
   - It should have the course teacher's signature.

## Process Assessment Classified Materials

Archive materials should be retained according to syllabus requirements. Examples listed by the policy:

| Category | Archive content | Quantity |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 考勤 | attendance requirements and scoring criteria | 1 copy per teaching class |
| 课堂表现 | classroom performance requirements and scoring criteria | 1 copy per teaching class |
| 笔记 | note requirements and scoring criteria | 5 students |
| 平时作业 | homework questions, requirements, and scoring criteria | 5 students |
| 实验 | experiment questions, requirements, and scoring criteria | 5 students |
| 期中测验 | test questions, requirements, and scoring criteria | 5 students |
| 实训作业 | practical training tasks, requirements, and scoring criteria | 5 students |
| 报告/论文 | topic, requirements, and scoring criteria | 5 students |

For a computer/project course, map actual evidence to these categories rather than forcing irrelevant categories.

## Archive Format and Timing

- All files should use PDF format for archive submission.
- Paper materials with grading marks, such as homework, exam papers, or handwritten reports, should be scanned and retained electronically. Originals should be kept by the college for inspection.
- Physical works are generally archived as image materials; if physical retention is required, the course unit specifies storage.
- Process assessment materials and final assessment materials should be archived at the same time.
- All course process and final assessment materials for the semester should be submitted to Academic Affairs by the college within two weeks after the next semester's make-up examination ends.

## Final Assessment Materials

For offline exam courses, an exam-paper bag includes:

1. `沧州交通学院考场登记表`;
2. `沧州交通学院考场记录单`;
3. `沧州交通学院成绩登记表`;
4. `沧州交通学院试卷分析报告`;
5. answers and scoring criteria;
6. student exam papers;
7. student answer sheets.

For online exam courses or scanned paper-review courses, all materials are submitted electronically in PDF format, including:

1. signed examination room registration form;
2. signed examination room record sheet;
3. grade registration form exported from the academic system and signed;
4. exam-paper analysis report exported from the academic system and signed;
5. sample papers A/B;
6. sample answers A/B;
7. examination materials exported from the online platform.

For other assessment forms such as practice training, course design, or experiments:

- The special bag should contain paper grade registration sheet and course assessment standard explanation.
- Specific assessment materials such as works and operation records are retained by the teaching college.
- Art courses including music should submit electronic versions of assessed works such as scanned copies or audio materials.
- Computer-operation courses should submit supporting materials for grade determination, formerly described as a supporting-material CD; in ordinary digital filing, keep an electronic support package containing reports, code, operation records, review records, and scoring evidence.
